## Narration master

**00:00–00:20** “Why has my energy changed? A chart can show a pattern, but it cannot responsibly tell me its cause. PHIS Open Agent turns that question into a bounded, inspectable investigation. Every record in this demo is fictional.”

**00:20–00:42** “I enter the question in the English interface. The agent checks the available 180-day window and missing observations, then chooses a scientific family and tools. Its plan and the executed steps stay visible.”

**00:42–01:05** “In this authenticated local Zetaris run, six real query pages supplied 1,080 fictional variable-day records. Each finding links back to source IDs. PHIS Discovery, a pre-existing deterministic scientific component, evaluates the records against frozen coverage, temporal and correction gates.”

**01:05–01:28** “The recent 28-day energy mean is about 0.35 points higher than the preceding 28 days. That is descriptive, not a causal explanation. The evidence panel distinguishes a qualified same-day association from a proven contributor and shows what remains uncertain.”

**01:28–01:50** “The official Meterless World Model Engine persists the verified context. When I ask what to investigate next, the follow-up reads that event and selects sleep. The new result has its own run and source IDs, rather than a copied answer.”

**01:50–02:12** “The verifier can also stop the agent. A clinical diagnosis question is outside this research scope: PHIS abstains, publishes no series and does not invent a measurement. This is a safety boundary you can inspect in the UI.”

**02:12–02:35** “In a separate synthetic local run, NVIDIA’s hosted Nemotron Lightning returned real response IDs for distinct energy and sleep plans and a critique of Discovery evidence. The model chose and checked steps; Discovery calculated the statistics. This is direct NVIDIA inference, not Meterless Power or Nightshift, and I am not combining it with the Zetaris footage as one run.”

**02:35–03:00** “The new hackathon work is the agent, Zetaris integration, World Model follow-up, evidence interface and model adapter. The five Discovery scientific modules existed before the event and are declared. PHIS is a synthetic research prototype: it does not diagnose, prove causation or ingest private health data. The repository and evidence ledger let judges reproduce and audit the result.”
