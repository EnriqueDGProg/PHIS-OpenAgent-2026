# Track 02 — The Agent That Can Explain Why

PHIS Open Agent helps a person investigate longitudinal energy or sleep changes using explicitly fictional records. It chooses bounded tools, carries source IDs into deterministic PHIS Discovery, checks returned evidence, and explains observations or abstains. A Meterless World Model event persists so a follow-up can select different work. Direct hosted NVIDIA Nemotron genuinely selected energy and sleep plans and critiqued a local synthetic Discovery result; it never authors statistics.

The [official track wording](https://hackathon.genai.works/event/open-agent-hackathon-2026) asks for agents that investigate complex questions, connect findings and produce evidence-backed answers. An organizer confirmed declared pre-existing components are eligible when the agentic application is built during the event; founder selected Track 02 in [PR #25](https://github.com/EnriqueDGProg/PHIS-OpenAgent-2026/pull/25#issuecomment-6383697898).

**PRE-EXISTING:** PHIS and exactly five PHIS Discovery v1.1 scientific modules at private baseline commit `516e3388ce67061028c1b1b29afb4a4ed965a2e0` predate 22 October. The founder authorized these exact modules under MIT; [NOTICE](../src/phis_master/NOTICE.md) identifies them. Original scientific methods are not hackathon inventions.

**NEW during the build window:** question-driven planner and orchestration, typed investigation API, Zetaris source adapter, official Meterless World Model persistence and follow-up, English evidence UI, fictional 180-day scenario and validation, and hosted Nemotron plan/critique adapter. World Model retains its separate Apache-2.0 upstream notice.

Six variables × 180 days yield 1,080 fictional variable-day records, not 1,080 people or independent days. Qualified associations are observational. No causal, diagnostic, treatment, automatic ingestion or real-world benefit claim is made. A consented real-data product and prospective usefulness study are future work.
