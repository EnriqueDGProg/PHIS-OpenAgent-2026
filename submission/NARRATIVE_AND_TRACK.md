## The problem: a real "why" question

People accumulate weeks or months of scattered daily observations about energy, sleep and habits, but an apparent relationship is not an explanation. A chatbot may turn a correlation into a confident story without checking missing observations, chronology, stability or multiplicity. PHIS Open Agent investigates the question **"What might be associated with changes in my energy over time?"** and exposes what the data support, what remains uncertain and when it must abstain.

## One exceptional end-to-end product experience

The judge asks an energy question and sees (1) a 180-day longitudinal series, missingness and the actual comparison period; (2) an investigation plan and data/tool selection; (3) source records fetched from an authenticated local Zetaris instance when in LIVE_SYNTHETIC_ZETARIS mode; (4) the deterministic PHIS Discovery analysis of predeclared candidate factors; (5) evidence verification and qualified associations or explicit abstention; (6) an official Meterless World Model Engine event that changes a subsequent sleep follow-up. Every run has trace and evidence IDs. The synthetic local mode runs science without external credentials and is never mislabeled as a real sponsor read.

**The agent** is the NEW product; **Discovery** is a declared pre-existing specialized scientific tool. An LLM may select bounded analysis work and critique evidence but NEVER calculates statistics, promotes unsupported hypotheses or infers causality. Both questions (energy and sleep) use fictional 180-day data. Neither synthetic validation nor an association demonstrates benefit for real patients.

## How judges can tell this is a strong agent instead of a pipeline

The demonstration MUST SHOW rather than merely label a feedback loop: the verifier constrains plan/tools or abstains when source/evidence is inadequate, or durable World Model context changes a later family's selection. A simulated agent dialogue, linear Agent A → B → C, or invented trace is not sufficient. The source ID, scientific event and persistent event must be connected in an inspectable real run. Fallback conditions are visible and not silently replaced with offline rows.

## Scientific + product claim discipline

- Distinguish observation vs association vs hypothesis vs causality; no diagnostic or treatment claims.
- Source includes six daily variables over 180 days (1,080 potential date-variable records), with missing values; **not 1,080 independent days or real patient observations**.
- Report selected hypothesis plan, family, missingness, temporal checks, uncertainty, negatives and limitations. Don't claim recent energy decline without the exact verified comparison window; old exploratory runs differed.
- Real user impact is a more interpretable investigation and actionable *next measurement/question*, NOT an experimentally proven healthcare outcome.
- Automated wearable ingestion, real health accounts and longitudinal prospective validation are ROADMAP.
