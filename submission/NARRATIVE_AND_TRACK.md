# The case for PHIS

**Proposed primary track, pending founder selection: The Agent That Can Explain Why.**

PHIS is designed for a question that a generic prompt cannot answer responsibly: **“Why has my energy changed lately?”** It investigates a longitudinal, explicitly synthetic record, exposes what it actually observed, checks candidate relationships using specialized deterministic Discovery, links statements to source records, and abstains when the evidence fails its gates. Its agent selects work in response to the question, coverage and prior state; the official Meterless World Model Engine persists a decision that can change a later follow-up. The scientific result remains separate from the language used to explain it.

## Why specialized deterministic Discovery matters

Discovery applies predeclared candidate families, temporal alignment, missingness and coverage checks, resampling, block sensitivity, hierarchical correction and temporal-validity gates. It can reject an attractive raw correlation. The agent may decide *which question and family to inspect next* and may explain the result, but it must not write measurements, confidence intervals or a causal conclusion. In PR #18, strong raw pairwise associations still failed promotion, and the right answer was an uncertainty/abstention state. The benchmark in PR #21 reports both an opportunity seed and a held-out seed without a qualified energy driver. These outcomes are a feature of honest scientific gating, not a demo defect to hide.

## Synthetic data: scope of proof

The demo data are fictional by construction. They can test typed records, missing-day handling, source-ID lineage, temporal windows, bounded queries, agent branching, memory readback, and whether the UI presents uncertainty and abstention correctly. The generator's equations are simulation design facts, isolated from the answering path. Synthetic outcomes **cannot** validate clinical benefit, generalize to people, establish a cause of low energy, show safe medical advice, prove two real health-provider integrations, or show automatic consented ingestion. Even a qualified association on one seed would remain an association in a constructed case.

The benchmark predeclared cases and seeds rather than selecting a pleasant result after looking. Reporting negative controls and the held-out failure makes the limits visible. A “negative-control unpromoted” result checks one failure mode; it does not prove global specificity, immunity to confounding, or correctness on all null cases. PR #21 explicitly notes that its null/confounded scenario includes a real stress pathway, so it is not pure-null evidence.

## Judge-facing line

> PHIS turns a personal “why” question into a traceable investigation. It can show an observed change, test candidate associations with a specialized scientific engine, and explain why a tempting finding was withheld. In this submission the records are synthetic, the live sponsor and local scientific proofs have different scopes, and clinical conclusions are out of scope.
